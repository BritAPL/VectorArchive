From: John R. Thorstensen

It was a surprise and delight to find (in an idle moment of web browsing) that APL Starmap is still alive and kicking on the Web!  I wrote that in the summer of 1973 between my 3rd and 4th years of College, when I happened to find lodging for a summer job in a spare bedroom at Paul and Sarita Berry’s house in Swarthmore, Pennsylvania. Since then I’ve gone on to other things ...
