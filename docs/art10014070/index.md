## Meetings

| Date | Location | Event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| June 25-28, 2001 | Yale University, New Haven, CT, USA | APL2001 |
| October 17-18, 2001 | World Trade Conference Center, New York | J User Conference |
| November 4-7, 2001 | Naples, Florida | APL2000 User Conference |
| July 22-25, 2002 | Madrid, Spain | APL2002 Conference |

<b>APL2001, Yale</b>  
The annual conference for 2001 is being held at Yale University (which is celebrating its 300th anniversary). For further details please see the [flyer](yale.htm) or visit the conference website at [www.acm.org/sigapl](http://www.acm.org/sigapl).

<b>J User Conference 2001</b>  
For details of the J User Conference please see the JSoftware website at [www.jsoftware.com](http://www.jsoftware.com) or email [Anne Faust](mailto:afaust@jsoftware.com).

<b>APL 2000 User Conference</b>  
For details please visit APL2000’s website at [www.apl2000.com/con01.htm](HTTP://www.apl2000.com/con01.htm).

<b>APL2002, Madrid</b>  
The annual conference for 2002 will be held at the Universidad Autonoma de Madrid, Spain. Professor Manuel Alfonseca is the Conference Chairman, and details will be posted on the website <tt><a href="http://www.apl2002.com">www.apl2002.com</a></tt> as they become available.
