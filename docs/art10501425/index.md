Minutes of the British APL Association AGM 2014 held on-line by webinar and at The Albion, 3 New Bridge Street, London EC4 on Friday 23 May 2014
{ .abstract }

<ol>    
  <li>
			The <strong>Minutes of the 2013 AGM</strong> (as published in Vector 26:1) were accepted by general consensus of those present.
  </li>
  
  <li>
  
    <p>
      <strong>Report from the Chairman</strong>
    </p>
    <p>
      With apologies from Paul Grosvenor, Peter Merrit (Acting Chair) presented the Chairman’s report on his behalf. 
    </p>
    <p>
      We continue to see some colour added into <cite>Vector</cite>, I hope everyone finds that a good thing to see.  
      The first BAPLA newsletter went out in December 2013 and are planning to send a further two throughout the year so if anyone has anything to announce then we are willing to include that as a service free to members.
    </p>
    <p>
      BAA London continues to meet regularly and my thanks to their team for allowing us to piggy back this AGM on their meeting.
    </p>
    <p>
      We have started to broadcast our meetings as webinars where appropriate and for those of you listening in today a special welcome. Its early days but the production team are trying and make this as easy as possible for everyone. We are hoping to publish some of the talks that are captured. Jake is in the process of editing those at the moment. 
    </p>
    <p>
      This year sees the thirtieth aniversary of the first publication of <cite>Vector</cite> which is quite an achievement. Later in the year we would like to publish a bumper edition and we would like as many BAPLA members as can to contribute. These contributions can take any form you like, one-liners; doodles; embarassing photographs; or old articles. It really does not matter this is your chance to get involved in making this quite an issue.
    </p>

    <p>
      Asked what were the deadlines for submission to <cite>Vector</cite>. John Jacob(Jake) confirmed that there wasn't a deadline as such rather that an issue of <cite>Vector</cite> depended on accumulating sufficient copy to send for print.
    </p>
    <p>
      Asked if <cite>Vector</cite> were available in PDF form. John Jacob confirmed that PDF version was made available to sustaining members at print time, with plans to make it publicly available at a later stage.
    </p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <p>
      <strong>Report from the Treasurer &amp; Membership Secretary</strong> (Nicholas Small)
    </p>
    <p>
      Very little change in our financial situation with total receipts just under £3,000 and payments just under £3,000. Cost of posting <cite>Vector</cite> overseas being the most significant part. 
      Chris Hogan (Auditor) confirmed the accounts. 
    </p>
    
    <p>
      Number of <cite>Vectors</cite> subscribed for is down by thirty-five. Five of these accounted for by Soliton ceasing subscription. The overall circulation of <cite>Vector</cite> was around 325 with about ten copies to libraries.
    </p>
    <p>
      Nicholas Small confirmed that dues for BAPLA membership fell due when at the end of a volume of <cite>Vector</cite>. 
    </p>
    <p>
      Peter Merit confirmed that a large package had been included in the Dyalog conference pack. 
    </p>
